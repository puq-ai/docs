# Account & Billing

Your account settings live under **Settings** in the left sidebar. From there you can manage your
profile and password, API keys, custom OAuth2 apps, encryption provider, and everything related to
billing — plan, balance, payment cards, and invoices.

## In this section

- [Profile & Password](#profile--password) — update your name, change your email, change your password
- [API Keys](/account/api-keys/) — create and revoke API keys used to call the puq.ai API
- [Billing](/account/billing/) — plans, balance, payment cards, coupons, and invoices
- [Startup Program](/account/startup-program/) — apply for discounted Enterprise access
- [Security](/security/) — encryption provider and how your data is protected
- [Connections → Custom OAuth2 Apps](/connections/custom-oauth2-apps/) — use your own OAuth2 client instead of puq.ai's default

## Profile & Password

Open **Settings → Account** to manage your profile. The page has two tabs:

- **Profile** — your name and email address. Click **Save Changes** to update your name. Your
  email is read-only here; click **Change** next to it to open the email change dialog.
- **Security** — change your account password. You must enter your **current password**, a
  **new password** (minimum 8 characters), and confirm it before clicking **Change Password**.

### Changing your email

Clicking **Change** on the Profile tab opens a dialog with two ways to change your email:

- **Enter Manually** — type a new email address, confirm your current password (if your account
  uses a password), and puq.ai sends a 6-digit verification code to the new address. Enter the
  code to complete the change. You can request a new code if it expires.
- **Sign in with Google** — pick a different Google account; its email replaces your current one
  instantly, without a verification code.

{: .note }
Some actions — creating a subscription, saving a payment card, and topping up your balance —
require a **verified email address**. If your email is not verified, puq.ai shows a banner with a
**Resend Email** button on the [Billing](/account/billing/) page.
