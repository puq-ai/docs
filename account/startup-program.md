# Startup Program

The Startup Program gives qualifying startups **6 months of free Enterprise plan** access. Apply
from `/startup-application` while signed in.

## Applying

The application form has four sections:

**Company Information**
- Company Name (required)
- Website
- Industry (required, select from a list)
- Country (required, select from a list)

**Startup Details**
- Founding Year (required)
- Funding Stage (required)
- Team Size (required)
- Monthly Revenue
- Company Size (required)

**Contact Information**
- Full Name (required)
- Email (required)
- Role
- Phone

**Requirements**
- How do you plan to use puq.ai? (required, up to 2000 characters)
- How did you hear about us?
- Current Tools

Full name and email are pre-filled from your account and can be edited. Click **Submit
Application** when done.

## After you apply

- You'll see a confirmation screen, and puq's team reviews applications within **2–3 business
  days**. You'll receive an email with the decision.
- While an application is **pending**, the page shows an **Application Under Review** screen
  instead of the form, and you cannot submit a second application.
- Once your application has been reviewed, you can submit a new one from the same page if needed.

{: .note }
You must be signed in to apply. The application is tied to your puq.ai account.
